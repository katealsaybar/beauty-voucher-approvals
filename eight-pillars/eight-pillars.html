Your journey

Four weeks. Four shifts.

Each week builds on the last. Small steps. Real change.

Week 1 · Foundation

The 8 Pillars. See yourself clearly.

Rate each pillar honestly. Find where you are struggling and where you are strong. Choose one focus. Take one small action.

Week 2 · Mindset

Rewire your thinking. The 80/20 rule.

Most of the change is mindset. Name the belief holding you back, replace it, and prove the new one with one small action.

Week 3 · Goals and action

Personal, professional, financial. The 2% rule.

Brainstorm freely, build your goal pyramid, then name what is in the way and turn it into a to-do list rather than a reason to stop.

Week 4 · Daily rhythm

Morning and evening.

How you begin your morning shapes your momentum. How you end your day shapes tomorrow. Build the practice.

“Small consistent actions compound. You do not need to change everything at once. You just need to start.”
