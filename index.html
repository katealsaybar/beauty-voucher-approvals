How the reminders work (client self-exit)

The nurture reminders are written as a kind reminder, never a "pay now" chase. Every email and WhatsApp message carries a client self-exit: an email button ("Already got yours? Tap here and we'll stop the reminders") and a WhatsApp quick-reply button or "DONE" keyword. When the client taps it, the sequence stops immediately and flags the contact centre to confirm the payment. This means we do not rely on the branch to report a sale before the reminders stop, and even if a message reaches someone who already paid at the branch, the soft tone keeps it from causing offence. This directly fixes the past complaints about payment-link emails over-sending.

Staff Incentive Proposal

Approve model
In one lineA per-sale bonus so reception and stylists actually push the voucher. Approve the model and budget.

The problem. Floor staff are hesitant to push the voucher because the prepaid-credit structure feels like it cuts their immediate commission. Without an incentive, the people closest to the client have no reason to recommend it.

Reassurance to give staff first. The voucher does not remove commission, it defers it. When a client later redeems her credit on a service, the stylist or beautician still earns their normal service commission at that visit. This incentive sits on top of that.

Recommended model — per-sale bonus, tiered

TierTotal bonusIntroducer (stylist)Closer (reception)
Dip Your ToesAED 50AED 20AED 30
Season of YouAED 150AED 60AED 90
All-In VIP YearAED 300AED 120AED 180
If one person both introduces and closes the sale, they get the full amount. This rewards the in-chair mention and the warm handoff, not just the till.
Alternative (last December's model): a flat 10% of voucher value to the attributed seller (AED 100 / 250 / 450). Pays more on high tiers but does not reward the stylist-to-reception handoff.

Rules and safeguards

  • Attribution is the staff member recorded on the Phorest sale, or named in the payment-link note. Disputes go to Kate, final call.
  • Paid on payment received, not on redemption. Refunds claw the bonus back.
  • Three Exits always (buy, gift, or come back next season). A pressured sale voids the bonus.
  • Mis-quoting the offer voids the bonus for that sale.
  • Runs for the campaign window only, purchases up to 28 August.
