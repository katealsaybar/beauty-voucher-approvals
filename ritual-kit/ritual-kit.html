How to read this
For reception and the front desk. Work top to bottom: has she done her mapping, then which voucher she bought (that sets how many items and the cap), then what she ticked on her map (that sets which line). Every kit below is priced off the live stock sheet, and every total is checked against the cap for you. The matrix is the same thing on one printable page for the desk drawer.

Which parts of her map you actually read. Confidence Mapping is not two stages, it runs Listen · Look · Colour · Live · You, then her map. The kit is built from three of them and you can ignore the rest: Stage 2, Look gives the concern that picks the bundle, Stage 3, Colour gives the henna and keratin flags, Stage 4, Live gives the detox override and the home-care opening. One answer sits outside that: “more shedding or thinning” is not one of the condition ticks, it is an option on the “have you noticed your hair changing recently?” question, and it raises a flag rather than picking a bundle.

The tree below now asks all three stages itself, in order, and puts every flag and override on the answer card automatically: henna and keratin sign-off, the shedding flag, and the Stage 4 detox override. The cap is staff information. It never goes to a client, and it never appears on a card in the bag. She is being given a kit matched to her hair. She is not being given a budget. Anything in a red-outlined box on this page stays behind the desk.
Standardised at 2 / 4 / 6 items. Six slots, filled in the same order every time, cumulative by tier. Same slots in both emirates, same cap in both emirates. What changes between Abu Dhabi and Dubai is only which product fills the slot, because the two emirates do not stock the same shelf.
Correction to what the pack said before. The earlier version of this page said the emirate does not change the kit. On doctrine that is still true, the prescription follows her hair. On stock it is wrong: the four branches share far less shelf than expected, so a single UAE-wide product list is not deliverable. Hence two lists below, one cap.

Do not bend these

The six hard rules

  1. No map, no kit. Her welcome email already told her the kit is personalised and released after her mapping. If we hand it over early we have made the mapping optional, and she will not book it.
  2. Fill the slots in order. Hero, then heat shield, then the wash pair, then the mask, then scalp or water defence. Never skip a slot to fit a nicer item into a later one.
  3. Every kit carries the heat shield. ABC Primer, AED 133, and it is on the shelf in all four branches. At Khalifa City it is ticketed as Flawless Primer. Same product, same price, different label.
  4. Every item carries a Because. Name the reason from her map and the consequence, then stop talking. Seven seconds. The silence is where she takes it in.
  5. It is home care, the insurance on her hair. Never “retail”, never “products”, never “an extra”. We prescribe, we do not sell up.
  6. Over the cap is not a judgement call. Swap an item down, or ask your manager. Do not hand over an over-cap bag and log it afterwards.
Found while pricing this

Six things in the stock sheet that need fixing

None of these blocks the kit. All of them will cause an argument later if nobody owns them. Emma.

WhatDetail
Three doctrine lines are not stocked at all Scalp Clinix, K18 and Fibreplex appear nowhere in the sheet, in any branch. The home care doctrine names all three as hero lines. Either the doctrine table is out of date or the sheet is. The bundles below route around them.
The water filter does not exist Doctrine says Full Insurance is not Full Insurance without a water filter. There is no filter in the sheet at any branch. Until one is sourced, the VIP kit uses a sixth item on scalp or detox instead, and we should stop describing a filter anywhere.
Khalifa City carries neither Fibre Clinix nor Olaplex Both are at the other three. Any kit built on those two lines is undeliverable at KCA, which is why the bond bundle is built on BlondMe Bond Repair instead, the one bond line priced in all four.
The same product is named differently per branch Primer spray 250ml against Flawless Primer 250ml, both 133. Detox Scalp Mud against Detoxifying Scalp Mud. Bond Repair Shampoo against Bond Rep Shampoo against Caviar Bond Rep Shampoo, all 210. On a straight name match only 19 items look common to all four branches. The real overlap is far higher, the names just drifted.
The same product is priced differently per branch Scalp Rituals Hemp 74ml is 170 at Al Quoz and 137 at Motor City, a 24 per cent gap inside one emirate. Dry Cleaning Spray Shampoo is 193 and 155. Detox Scalp Mud is 302 and 289. A client who visits both branches will notice.
The Category column is auto-filled and wrong in places Nail products sit under Hair Mask/Treatment, cuticle oil under Hair oil. Fine for stock, useless for filtering, so every pick here was checked by product name rather than trusting the column.

If an item is out of stock

  1. Substitute within the same slot and the same purpose, and re-check the total against the cap.
  2. Tell her before she opens it, and tell her why. A swap she finds herself reads as a downgrade. A swap she is told about reads as care.
  3. Log it, so her next kit does not repeat it and the stock gap is visible.
What you actually say

The Because, by tier

Fill the bracket from her map. Then stop talking. The pause is doing the work, not the sentence.

Dip Your Toes · handing over Protect “These two are your shields. I am giving you these because your hair is [her reason], and without them what we do today starts coming undone in two to three weeks. Your shampoo and conditioner at home are still doing their own thing, so this is the floor, not the finish.”
Season of You · handing over Protect and Maintain “This is your full routine, matched to your hair, working every time you wash. The shampoo and conditioner you have at home are almost certainly undoing what we do in the chair. Swap those two and the result holds between visits instead of fading. This is where most women start.”
All-In VIP Year · handing over Full Insurance “This is the complete insurance. On top of your routine, the weekly mask rebuilds the hair, and the sixth is for your scalp, because everything else works better when the scalp is settled. So every visit builds on the last, rather than starting again.”

Never say

Retail. Products. An extra. Premium. Upsell. Guarantee. And never the value of the bag, in either direction. If she asks what it is worth, the answer is what it is for.
