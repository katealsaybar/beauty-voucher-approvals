Home Ritual Kit
What your answers unlock

Your Home Ritual Kit is built from Stage 2.

Every Beauty Voucher includes a Home Ritual Kit, and it is not a standard bag handed to everybody. What goes in it is chosen from what you ticked about your own hair, which is why we cannot make it up before you have been mapped. Do the mapping and it is put together for you.

1 · Your heroThe one item the whole kit is built around, matched to what you ticked at Stage 2.
2 · Heat shieldIn every kit, every tier. Heat, sun and friction are the everyday things that undo a result.
3 · Your shampooMatched to the same answer, because the wash is half of the routine and the half nobody thinks about.
4 · Your conditionerThe pair to it. Splitting the pair is the commonest way a routine quietly stops working.
5 · Weekly maskSits on top of the daily routine, so the result builds rather than only holds.
6 · Scalp or water defenceThe scalp is the skin your hair grows from. Settle it and everything above it works better.

How many of the six are in your kit depends on the voucher you hold: two on Dip Your Toes, four on Season of You, six on the All-In VIP Year. They are always filled in that order, so the first thing in the bag is always the thing your hair needs most.

Products differ between our Abu Dhabi and Dubai salons, because the shelves differ. The kit does not.
