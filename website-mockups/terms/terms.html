  6. New term Colour: which services your voucher reaches. Colour follows the salon’s own three-step plan ladder, and each voucher reaches one step further than the one below it. Every voucher includes everything on the steps beneath it. A colour service above your step can still be booked and settled in the usual way, outside your credit.

Cuts, blow-dry and styling, keratin and smoothing, and bond, strength, hydration and scalp treatments are covered on every voucher and are not part of this ladder. There is no per-visit spending cap on colour of any kind: this term is a ladder of services, not a limit on an amount. Where a service is not named above, the salon will tell you which step it falls on before your appointment, never at the till.

Physical gifts are subject to availability; Tara Rose may substitute an unavailable complimentary item with an alternative of similar value.
  8. New · 18 Aug · kit becomes an allowance Home Ritual Kit allowance. Each voucher includes a home care allowance towards your Home Ritual Kit, set by tier: AED 100 with Dip Your Toes, AED 200 with Season of You and AED 450 with All-In VIP Year. The allowance is redeemable against home care, the take-home products recommended for you, at the salons named on your voucher, and where the home care recommended for you comes to more than the allowance, the difference is payable when you collect your kit. We tell you the difference before anything is packed, never after. If you would rather keep it as small as possible, tell us before your kit is packed and we will build it to the fewest items that will work. The allowance is separate from your service credit: it cannot be spent on services, and your service credit cannot be spent on home care. Any unused part of the allowance has no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and ends with the validity period of your voucher.
  9. Reworded · matches Phorest Gift vouchers. The gift vouchers included with your Wellness Voucher are issued digitally and sent by email, in your name, to the people you nominate. Nothing is posted or printed. Dip Your Toes includes one card of AED 100, Season of You three cards of AED 100, and All-In VIP Year five cards of AED 100. Each card can be redeemed against an eligible salon service at the same participating salons named in term 2, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be used to purchase retail products or another voucher. Each card is valid for two months from the date you buy your Wellness Voucher, not from the date you pass it on, so give it to your friend early: a card handed over late will have less than two months left on it. The expiry date is printed on the card. The main Wellness Voucher credit cannot be transferred to another person unless otherwise agreed by Tara Rose.
  10. Referral rewards. Referral credit is awarded when the required number of new clients successfully visit Tara Rose and complete a qualifying paid appointment. Referral credit is issued once the referral is complete, meaning the new client has visited and paid, and is then valid for two months from that date. It runs on its own clock rather than expiring with your Wellness Voucher, so referring a friend late in your validity period does not shorten what you earn. Referral rewards cannot be exchanged for cash.
  11. Reworded per emirate Birthday benefits. Birthday services must be redeemed during the validity period of your voucher and are subject to appointment availability. In Abu Dhabi the birthday facial is a full facial of your choice from our Abu Dhabi menu. In Dubai the birthday facial is the Signature Relaxing Facial with lifting massage at Al Quoz. The values shown within the campaign represent the standard menu value of the relevant service and are included to illustrate the overall voucher value.
  12. Settled · 19 August Appointments, cancellations & no-shows. All appointments remain subject to Tara Rose's standard booking, cancellation and no-show policies. Voucher ownership or priority access does not guarantee appointment availability with a particular stylist, therapist, date or time. Dawn's wording, chosen over the longer version on 19 August. One policy, written once, in the place the salon already keeps it, so a change to the live policy can never leave these terms behind. The Cancellation Policy section of the approval pack comes out to match.
  13. Expiry. Your service credit and bonus credit must be redeemed within the stated validity period for the Wellness Voucher you bought: six months on Dip Your Toes, nine months on Season of You and twelve months on All-In VIP Year, each running from the date of purchase. Two benefits run on their own clocks and are not covered by that period: the gift cards for your friends, which last two months from the date you buy, and referral credit, which lasts two months from the date a referral completes. Any unused balance or promotional benefits remaining after the relevant expiry date will expire and cannot be exchanged for cash or extended unless Tara Rose agrees otherwise.
  14. Refunds & exchanges. Wellness Vouchers are non-refundable once purchased, except where otherwise required by applicable law. Voucher credit and promotional bonuses cannot be exchanged for cash.
  15. Other offers. Wellness Voucher credit, bonuses and complimentary benefits cannot be combined with another promotion, discount or offer unless Tara Rose specifically confirms otherwise.
  16. Tabby payments. Where Tabby is offered, approval and payment arrangements are provided directly by Tabby and are subject to Tabby's eligibility requirements and terms. Using Tabby does not change the amount of Tara Rose service credit included within the selected Wellness Voucher.
  17. Pricing. All prices and values shown are in UAE Dirhams (AED) and include applicable VAT.
  18. General. Tara Rose reserves the right to reasonably amend or clarify these terms where required for the administration of the campaign, provided that this does not remove service credit already purchased by a client.
