The Wellness Voucher

For: all stylists & beauticians
Purchases close: 30 September 2026
Dubai & Abu Dhabi · all branches
Say it exactly like this

“It’s not money off. You place your credit, we add more on top, and it becomes your plan for the year.”

1 What it actually is

The client pays her own money in. We add extra credit on top. She spends it with us over 6, 9 or 12 months, depending on which plan she bought.

Reception knows which plan she is on. Ask before you book her colour.

She may call her plan by its name rather than by a number of months. If she does, do not guess which one it is. Reception will tell you.

2 Your commission

It is not taken away. It moves to when she spends the credit. You still get paid.

3 Four things you never say

  • “Free” : she paid for it herself.
  • “Unlimited” : the credit is a limit, not a free pass.
  • “Your kit is included” : say towards. She always pays a difference.
  • Any number for colour : colour is a ladder, not a budget.

4 Colour, check her plan first

All plans: roots, single-process colour, toner, gloss
9 & 12 month plans: the above, plus balayage, freehand highlights, grey blending
12 month plan only: the above, plus colour correction, high lift, big change

If she wants something above her plan, have that conversation before the appointment, never as a surprise at the till.

5 Home kit, price before you pack

She has an allowance towards her kit. It is not a free kit. Say the number out loud first, every time.

Word for word “You’ve got AED 450 towards your kit, your products came to AED 910, so that’s AED 460 today.”

No kit leaves the salon until her Confidence Mapping is done and the difference is paid.

6 What the credit does not cover

  • Home care and retail products
  • New hair extensions

Her kit allowance is separate from her credit. Her credit never pays for home care.

Everything else is fair game, within her plan.

7 Her credit is safe

Cancellations, reschedules, running late, no-shows: none of it touches her voucher balance. Normal salon booking rules still apply to everything else.

Never quote her an end date. Dates are not the same on every plan. Reception owns dates.

8 After 30 September

Purchases close. Nothing else changes. Every rule on this page still applies for as long as her plan is running, which for some clients is well into 2027.

If you are not sure, ask before you tell the client. A wrong answer on the floor costs us far more than a five-minute question. Any stylist who doesn’t know this campaign is where we get caught out.

Reception first. If reception is not sure, your branch manager. Nobody guesses.
