2The build order, and why it is this order

Item 1 can invalidate everything under it, so do not start at the bottom.

  • Wait for LID to build the bridge. Do not start below this line until it exists. Answered 19 August: the Confidence Mapping form sends its entries straight to info@tararosesalon.com as an email notification. That is a Gravity Forms notification, not an integration, so no Confidence Mapping answer has ever reached GHL. The webhook is LID’s job, assigned 19 August, along with the branch question on the same form. Chase it, because everything under this item is waiting on it. See section 3.
  • Create the emirate custom field. A field, not only a tag. Merge fields cannot read tags, and the emails have to read the same value.
  • Create emirate_source. So a wrong guess and a wrong client answer can be told apart later.
  • Build the five routing nodes. If/Else · Emirate known? and WhatsApp · Which emirate? in Lead Nurture. If/Else · Emirate from payment and Task · Emirate mismatch in the Welcome Pack. If/Else · Locale to emirate in Confidence Mapping.
  • Fill the three custom values. {{voucher_availability_line}}, {{branch_a_name}} and {{branch_b_name}} are already written into the emails and render blank today.
  • Create the nineteen Confidence Mapping fields and map them, one at a time. Nothing maps for free on a Gravity Form.
  • Verify with a single test submission, read at both ends.

3The form goes to an inbox, not to GHL

Answered by Kate on 19 August, and it is the worst of the three cases the pack allowed for. The fix is LID’s, but the consequence is yours, so read it before starting on field mappings.

The quiz posts to info@tararosesalon.com by email notification. Nothing else happens. That means:

  • Mapping leads are not contacts. They exist as emails in a shared inbox. GHL has never seen them.
  • The Confidence Mapping workflow has never fired for a real lead. Thirteen steps, sitting on a trigger nothing reaches.
  • Nothing is searchable, deduplicated or attributable. A woman who filled the form twice is two emails. A woman who filled it and then bought cannot be joined up.
  • Every answer she gave is only in an email body. Nineteen answers about her hair, which is the whole point of the exercise, and no stylist can see them at the chair.

So the first build is not a mapping, it is a hop, and it is not yours. A webhook notification from Gravity Forms into a GHL inbound webhook is the smallest thing that works, it goes in alongside the existing email rather than replacing it, and LID owns it as of 19 August. Your half starts the moment an entry lands: the nineteen custom fields, the mapping, the routing nodes.

What LID is shipping on that form, so you know what to expect. Three notification changes and one build: her name, contact details and branch go into the info@ email; the client gets her own copy of her answers; a branch question is added; and then the webhook. The branch field is the one to care about, because it is the emirate signal every workflow here has been missing.
The email notification stays when the bridge goes in. The contact centre works from that inbox today, so the webhook is added beside it, not instead of it. If you are asked to switch the email off as part of the cutover, say no.
Verify together, with one test submission read at both ends. Neither half proves anything on its own, and a mapping that looks right against a form that never fired is the failure mode here.

4Lead Nurture takes two entrances

Settled by Kate, 19 August. This was the open question that blocked the build.

Both the website reserve form and a broadcast to a segmented Phorest list feed the same workflow.

  • Form entrants carry a hidden emirate field, so the emails are correct from the first message.
  • Broadcast entrants cannot carry one. Their emirate comes from Phorest, and where Phorest does not have it, from the WhatsApp ask inside Lead Nurture.

This is the largest of the three builds: two emirate paths, and five emails that have to read as well to a client we already know as to a new lead. Stage it. The window is already open, so this lands in pieces on a live campaign, not as one launch.

Broadcast entrants skip the Day 0 WhatsApp. Decided 19 August. The blast WhatsApp and the Day 0 WhatsApp are the same message to the same woman, and Meta delivers one marketing message per contact per 24 hours. A broadcast entrant has just had the blast, so her Day 0 is the one Meta drops, and nothing logs an error: the arc opens on a message that never arrived. Gate the step. Broadcast entrant, or wellness:blast-wa already on the contact, skip the WhatsApp and carry on to the Day 0 email. Form entrant, send it as written, because she never had the blast.
A second template does not fix this, so nobody spends a day submitting one. The 24 hour cap is per contact, not per template. The only lever is timing, and with the window this short, skipping the duplicate beats holding the whole arc back a day. The blast is the message that has buttons and a tested character count; the Day 0 is the one to give up.
Two dead ends, so nobody re-proposes them. The emirate cannot be guessed from the phone number, because +971 covers the whole country. And the payment is a reliable emirate signal but an unreliable branch one, because Saadiyat has no working Stripe and its clients pay through the Khalifa City link.

5The traps that fail silently

The tier is not recorded on in-salon sales

tier:[x] is written by the Stripe bridge only. A till or Tabby sale gets voucher:paid applied by hand and no tier, and most purchases are expected to happen in salon. Three things then break, none of which logs an error: the Self-Care Bonus paragraph vanishes from Welcome Email 2, the expiry touch cannot work out whether to fire at 5, 8 or 11 months, and the 8 Pillars Journal never sends because it is gated on tier:vip.
Not a naming collision, before anyone goes looking. tier:[x] carries two meanings, the voucher tier from payment (dip / season / vip) and the home care tier from Confidence Mapping (protect / maintain / full). The two value sets do not overlap, so tier:vip is unambiguous. The fault is absence, not ambiguity, and renaming nothing fixes it.

WhatsApp templates over the Meta limits

Going over does not get trimmed, it gets refused. Meta caps an outbound template at 1,024 characters and each button label at 25. Over the limit, Meta rejects it at approval, the step stays in the workflow with nothing to send, and there is no warning at the moment it would have gone out. Our Abu Dhabi broadcast reached 1,193 characters once, and two button labels reached 26. The blast now sits at 894 because the link moved into a URL button — see §6 for all four bodies and their counts.
WhatsApp stays split by emirate and cannot be parametrised, because Meta fixes button labels at template level and they will not take a merge field. The blast is split the same way, and split again for the lists where the first name is unusable. The emails do not need splitting: three strings change, the body of the Day 4 nurture email, and the body and hidden preheader of Welcome Email 2.

Confidence Mapping locale is not the emirate

An earlier note said locale was “the emirate already collected and thrown away.” It is not. The live script sets locale from the page language attribute, so it reads en-us for every lead and always will. There is nothing to recover. Mapping leads stay emirate:unknown until the branch question ships, and take it from the WhatsApp ask until then.

7Live compliance issue, today

The consent tickbox on the Confidence Mapping form is still set to required. That is a PDPL problem and it is live right now, not a launch task.

Two more things on the same form that nobody has listed. “Journal” appears in two client-facing places: the consent label, and the submit button, which reads “Send my Journal”. 8Message rules that are not preferences

  • Every message carries a client self-exit. An email button (“Already got yours? Tap here and we’ll stop the reminders”) and a WhatsApp quick reply or the keyword DONE. It stops the sequence immediately and flags the contact centre to confirm the payment.
  • No message asks her to compose a reply. Every WhatsApp template carries tappable buttons, which is also how the workflow knows what to do next.
  • The payment confirmation offers her own two salons only, plus “Help me choose”. An earlier version listed all four, which left a Dubai client looking at two salons she cannot use.
  • Email footers read “Tara Rose Salons”, not “Tara Rose Salon Group”. The old build spec said otherwise and the build spec is not the source of truth.

9Two things to hand back before the campaign closes

The waits are fixed-date for this window. They need rebuilding as relative delays once the campaign is over, or the workflows are dead the moment the dates pass.

The expiry touch must survive the campaign closing. It fires 30 days before a tier lapses, which for a 12-month voucher is well into next year. That is where unredeemed-credit complaints live, so it cannot be switched off with the campaign.

Until a Phorest to GHL webhook exists, reception applies voucher:redeemed at checkout by hand. Worth building, but it is not blocking.
