2The Confidence Mapping form, and why all of it is yours

Assigned to LID on 19 August. The webhook had been sitting with the GHL team, who cannot start their half until it exists.

What actually happens today

The quiz is Gravity Forms 21 on WordPress. It is not a GHL-native form and it does not post to GHL. Its only notification is an email to info@tararosesalon.com carrying her answers. That is the whole integration.

What that costs. Mapping leads are not contacts, so GHL has never seen one. The thirteen-step Confidence Mapping workflow has never fired for a real lead. A woman who filled the form twice is two emails. A woman who filled it and then bought a voucher cannot be joined up. And her nineteen answers, which are the entire point of the exercise, are readable only by whoever opens the inbox.

The smallest thing that works is a webhook notification from Gravity Forms into a GHL inbound webhook. 9House rules for anything you write

  • No em-dashes, anywhere. Universal ban, no exemptions, including code comments. Use commas, full stops or colons.
  • British English. Colour, favourite, personalised, organise, programme.
  • Banned in client copy: free, unlimited, premium, luxury as hype, guarantee, upsell, pamper, treat yourself, discount, % off, exclamation marks, the rose emoji.
  • “VIP” and “Ritual” are allowed on this campaign only, as the locked names: All-In VIP Year, Home Ritual Kit. They are banned on every other Tara Rose asset.
  • Client-facing brand name is “Tara Rose”. Never TRS, never TRG. Footer signature: “We see you.”
  • One more question is needed on the Confidence Mapping quiz: her emirate. It is a website change, so ship it with the emirate landing-page split rather than on its own.
