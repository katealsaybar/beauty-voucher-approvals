The 8 Pillars of Wellness is a four-week journal. Not a course, and nothing to keep up with. Four weeks of looking honestly at where you are, one week at a time.

Four weeks, four shifts
Week one · Foundation
Rate your eight pillars and see where you are strong, and where you are running on empty.
Week two · Mindset
Name the belief that keeps getting in the way, and choose the one you would rather hold.
Week three · Goals
Personal, professional, financial. What is in the way becomes a to-do list rather than a reason to stop.
Week four · Daily rhythm
A morning and an evening you can actually keep. Small, and yours.

The eight pillars are physical, emotional, financial, spiritual, occupational, intellectual, social and environmental. You score each one out of ten, and there is one rule: you cannot use the number seven. Seven is the easy hiding place. Either something is a struggle, six or below, or it is a strength, eight or above. Choosing is the whole exercise.

This is not about fixing yourself. It is about seeing yourself clearly.
